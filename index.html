About

Research at the intersection of sequences, graphs, and science

I am a PhD student in AI at Università della Svizzera italiana (USI) and IDSIA, working with the Graph Machine Learning Group. My research focuses on Time Series Foundation Models, including Mamba, S4, and S5 architectures, as well as Graph Neural Networks for structured forecasting problems.

I am especially interested in ecological modeling applications, such as predicting insect spread across landscapes using temporal, spatial, and environmental signals. My background in engineering and signal processing shapes how I approach scalable, interpretable, and scientifically grounded machine learning systems.

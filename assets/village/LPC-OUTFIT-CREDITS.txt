Shared body: Masculine, Thin; head: Head 02 - Masculine; shoes: Shoes 01, Brown.
Felix: Shirt 01 - Longsleeve Shirt, Cream; Pants 05 - Overalls, Blue;
Hair Short 02 - Parted, Brown.
Tomas: Shirt 01 - Longsleeve Shirt, Red; Pants 03 - Pants, Navy;
Hair Short 06 - Balding, Gray.

For each outfit the layer order is body, shoes, shirt, pants/overalls, head, hair.
All source cells are 64x64, with direction rows north, west, south, east.
Walking uses eight columns 0–7. Idle holds column 0. Sitting holds only column 2,
the actual chair/bench pose with bent knees. Sitting columns 0–1 are ground
postures and are not used. The frame anchor is (32,62), with hips near (32,46).

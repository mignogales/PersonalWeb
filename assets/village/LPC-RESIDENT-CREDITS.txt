LPC VILLAGE RESIDENTS — ARTWORK ATTRIBUTION

The source PNG layers are supplied unchanged. village-sprites.mjs assembles
and rearranges their frames at runtime into walking and fishing animations.
The assembled resident artwork is licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
This artwork license applies to the assembled character art, not the site code.

FISHING ROD
lpc-rod-bg.png and lpc-rod-fg.png by bluecarrot16.
https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-hand-tools
Used under CC BY 4.0, as listed for the fishing rod in the upstream hand-tool
credits. Original notices preserved in LPC-HAND-TOOLS-CREDITS.txt and
LPC-FISHING-CREDITS.txt. The character thrust frames are reordered using
the author's documented 13-frame mapping, then layered between rod-bg and rod-fg.
